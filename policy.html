We do not collect sensitive personal data (as defined in Article 9 GDPR), and we do not engage in automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the GDPR, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): For server-access logs collected by the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) necessary for the security and operation of the Website.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): For placing the cookie consent preference cookie on your device. You may withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): For processing email correspondence, to respond to your enquiry and manage the business relationship.

5. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. This Website uses only one first-party cookie:

  • Name: cookiesAccepted
  • Purpose: Stores your cookie consent choice (accepted or declined) so the banner is not shown on every visit.
  • Type: Functional / strictly necessary for consent management.
  • Duration: Persists until you clear your browser's local storage.
  • Third-party access: None. This value is stored in your browser's localStorage and is never transmitted to any server.

We do not use analytics cookies (e.g. Google Analytics), advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or any third-party cookies. Google Fonts are loaded from Google's CDN; this may cause your browser to make a request to Google's servers, which may process your IP address under Google's own privacy policy. No cookies are set by this request.

You can remove the consent cookie at any time by clearing your browser cookies or localStorage. You can also configure your browser to refuse all cookies; however, this may affect the functioning of some websites you visit.

  • Right to rectification (Art. 16): You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17): You may request deletion of your personal data where there is no legitimate reason to continue processing it.
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18): You may request that we restrict the processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20): Where processing is based on consent or contract and carried out by automated means, you may request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)): Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

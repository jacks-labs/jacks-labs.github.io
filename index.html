Jaka Bevk is the founder of Jacks Labs — a hardware and embedded systems engineering consultancy based in Slovenia, central Europe.

With 16+ years of hands-on development experience, Jaka specialises in complex connected electronics: multilayer HDI PCB design, wireless integration (LTE/WiFi/BLE/GNSS), embedded firmware, and taking products from initial concept through to volume production.

Prior to founding Jacks Labs, Jaka spent 11 years at U-Blox as a Senior Engineer in Module Design, taking part in volume development of cellular IoT modules (LTE-M/NB-IoT) and building production flashing, testing infrastructure, and coexistence systems for multi-radio platforms.

Independent work spans industrial IoT gateways, UAV autopilot electronics, agricultural robotics, consumer smart devices, and safety training systems — several of which have reached volume deployment across Europe.

Open for project-based collaboration, consulting engagements, and long-term technical partnerships.
